Air conditioners in Aventura, Hollywood and Fort Lauderdale do not get a long off-season. They cool and dehumidify almost every day. That run time is why a duct-cleaning interval copied from a colder climate is only a starting point.

## A reasonable baseline

For most homes, **every 3 to 5 years** is a sound time to clean supply and return ducts. The ducts are not a filter. They are a path. Dust, dander and debris that get past the filter settle in that path and can blow back into rooms when the system starts.

## Book sooner if any of these are true

- You just finished a renovation, and drywall dust is still showing up.
- Pets live in the home, and returns load up quickly.
- You can see debris inside a supply vent.
- Someone in the household is more comfortable with the windows open than with the AC on.
- It has been longer than five years, or nobody knows the last time it was done.
- A musty smell shows up when the system starts. That one may also need [HVAC cleaning](/hvac-cleaning/), because the odor often starts on a wet coil rather than in the duct.

## Condos and houses are not the same job

Aventura and Sunny Isles towers usually have a closet air handler and short runs. Houses in North Miami Beach, Pembroke Pines and Coral Springs often have longer flex duct in a hot attic. Both should be cleaned. The setup, the time on site and what we can inspect are different, so say which one you have when you book.

## What cleaning does not do

Duct cleaning removes debris from the air path. It does not repair a crushed or disconnected run, it does not recharge refrigerant, and it is not a mold test. If one room never cools, start with an [air duct inspection](/air-duct-inspection/) so you are not paying to clean a duct that is no longer connected.
