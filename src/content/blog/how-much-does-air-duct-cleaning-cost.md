Air duct cleaning cost is the question we hear before almost anything else. A national figure is only a starting point. The U.S. Environmental Protection Agency says these services typically run **$450 to $1,000 per heating and cooling system**, depending on what is included, the size of the system, how easy it is to reach, and how dirty it is. Consumer guides often show a lower band, around $270 to $500, for a straightforward single-system home.

Neither number is your quote. A closet air handler in an Aventura condo and a two-story house in Pembroke Pines are different jobs.

## What changes the price

- **How many systems.** One air handler is one cleaning. A house with upstairs and downstairs units is two.
- **How many vents.** Supply registers and return grilles are part of the job. A small condo has fewer of them than a large house.
- **Access.** A hallway closet is simpler than flex duct buried in a hot attic, and a high-rise can add elevator rules, loading docks, and parking.
- **How dirty it is.** Renovation dust, pet hair, and years of buildup take longer than a system that was cleaned recently.
- **What else is on the visit.** [HVAC cleaning](/hvac-cleaning/) for a matted coil, [air duct sanitizing](/air-duct-sanitizing/) after the dust is out, or [dryer vent cleaning](/dryer-vent-cleaning/) on the same trip are separate lines on the quote.

Ask for the total in writing before work starts, including which ducts, registers, and equipment are included. The EPA recommends a written scope for that reason. An hourly rate with no end point is how a simple visit turns into a surprise.

## Condos and houses are priced differently

Towers in Aventura, Sunny Isles Beach, and Hallandale Beach usually have one closet air handler and short runs. The cleaning itself is contained. The time is often in getting equipment to the unit and working within building rules.

Houses in North Miami Beach, Hollywood, Plantation, and Coral Springs more often have longer flex duct in the attic, and sometimes a second system. That is more duct, more vents, and more time on site. [Commercial air duct cleaning](/commercial-air-duct-cleaning/) for an office or shop is quoted by the systems in the space, and it is often scheduled before the business opens.

## What a very low price usually means

A coupon that quotes one flat number for “whole house ducts” without asking how many systems you have is guessing. So is a visit that only wipes the grille you can see. Air duct cleaning, vent cleaning, and ductwork cleaning are the supply and return runs, the registers, and the accessible plenums. Wiping the cover is housekeeping.

If one room is weak because a flex run is crushed or disconnected, cleaning will not fix it. That finding belongs on an [air duct inspection](/air-duct-inspection/), and the repair is [AC duct replacement](/ac-duct-replacement/).
